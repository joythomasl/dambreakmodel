Terrain: Mapzen Terrain Tiles/AWS Open Data. Satellite: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2024 by EOX. Roads and structures: OpenStreetMap contributors.

SI units: horizontal distance and elevation in metres (m), elevation relative to mean sea level where available, water depth in metres (m), and elapsed time in minutes (min) or hours (h). Terrain and water height use the selected vertical exaggeration; colours are visual classes, not extra model resolution. The numerical engine approximates depth-averaged shallow-water flow on 50 m cells. Not suitable for warnings, evacuation or final cost decisions.
